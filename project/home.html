How a reservation works

Four steps from search to parking spot.

  1. Search

    Choose a city and, if you like, the times you need.

  2. Choose

    See price, free spaces and distance. Full places say so.

  3. Pay

    Pay securely online. Your reservation is confirmed once the payment is verified.

  4. Park

    Find your reservation in My dashboard and open the place on the map.
